1 · Tasks

Each card is one downstream task: what it monitors, what is detected or which classes are told apart, and the public datasets used.

Target detection (visual ERP) →

Detection
Watches the brain’s reaction to symbols flashing on a screen and spots the rare target symbol.

Detects: the response to a target flash (‘OOO’, 1 in 5 flashes) versus a non-target flash (‘XXX’).

Figure: Lee et al., Sci. Data 2021, Fig. 1d, CC BY 4.0, cropped

Lee et al. 2021 — Mobile BCI
ear-EEG 14 ch · scalp 15 ch · standing

Attended-word detection →

Detection
Two people talk at once; the listener follows one of them. For each spoken word, decide whether it came from the talker the listener is following.

Detects: a word from the attended talker versus a word from the ignored talker.

Figure: Nguyen, Mikkelsen & Kidmose, Front. Hum. Neurosci. 2025, Fig. 1b, CC BY 4.0, cropped

Aarhus ear-EEG / scalp dataset
scalp 32 ch (official) · 16 ch (EEGNet grid)

Gaze target from flicker (SSVEP) →

Classification3 classes
Three boxes flicker at different rates; the brain’s rhythm follows the box the person looks at. Tell which box it is, standing or walking.

Classes:

  • box flickering at 5.45 Hz
  • box flickering at 8.57 Hz
  • box flickering at 12 Hz

Figure: Lee et al., Sci. Data 2021, Fig. 1e, CC BY 4.0, cropped

Epileptic seizure detection →

Detection
Watches continuous recordings from two small electrodes behind the ears and flags when a seizure is happening.

Detects: a focal epileptic seizure versus no seizure, for every 2-second window.

Figure: Bhagubai et al., Sci. Data 2025, Fig. 2, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, unmodified

SeizeIT2 — wearable focal epilepsy
behind-the-ear 2 ch

2 · Accuracy surfaces

One panel per task; inside it, one block per dataset or electrode setup; inside each block, one plot per training / adaptation method. Pick any two of channels / Hz / bits as the x and y axes; the third is fixed by its buttons. Height = metric on a fixed 0–1 scale (mean over the tested channel subsets of that size; hover for the best subset). The red plane is the chance level of the task. Drag to rotate · scroll to zoom · double-click to enlarge. Switch a task to “Compare” to overlay 2–4 chosen plots (any dataset · model · method of that task) in one large cube, or show the difference of two.
