The wearable EEG tasks shown on this page and the public dataset behind each one.

Target detection (ERP)

Detection
Picks up the brain’s response when a rare target symbol flashes on screen among frequent non-target symbols. Each flash is labelled target or non-target.

Lee et al. 2021 — Mobile BCI (ERP)
ear-EEG 14 ch · scalp 15 ch

Gaze target from flicker (SSVEP)

Classification3 classes
Tells which of three flickering boxes the person is looking at, from the brain’s rhythm that follows the flicker rate. Works standing and while walking.

Lee et al. 2021 — Mobile BCI (SSVEP)
ear-EEG 14 ch · scalp 8 ch · standing & walking

Figure: Lee et al., Sci. Data 2021, Fig. 1e, CC BY 4.0, cropped
